Farmgate Collection & Moisture Testing

Field agronomists test moisture directly at the cooperative collection point using calibrated grain probes. Any lot exceeding allowable moisture or displaying insect damage is rejected before loading.

✓ Moisture Guaranteed ≤ 13% • ✓ 100% Cooperative Origin Traceability

Air-Screen Destoning & Optical Sortex

Mechanical destoning eliminates stones, chaff, and heavy debris. High-speed optical sortex cameras inspect grain color and geometry, isolating discolored kernels for an unblemished commercial batch.

✓ 0.0% Stone Contamination • ✓ Foreign Matter < 0.5%

Weighbridge Calibration & Net Weight Guarantee

We eliminate shortage leakage entirely. Every dispatch is weighed on digital commercial weighbridges with signed weight slips. Every 50kg or 90kg sack is machine calibrated.

✓ Net Weight Manifest • ✓ Certified Scale Guarantee

Dedicated Fleet Delivery & Warehouse Offloading

Tarp-sealed, GPS-tracked freight trucks transport orders directly from processing plants to your silos. Our crew coordinates offloading with your facility team for zero downtime.

✓ 24-48 Hour Scheduled Dispatch • ✓ On-Site Offloading Support

Institutional Term Contracts & Price Locking

Protect your budget against seasonal price spikes. We offer 3-month, 6-month, and annual term contracts for boarding schools, healthcare complexes, and industrial millers with guaranteed buffer stock.

✓ Fixed Spot Rates • ✓ Priority Harvest Allocation
